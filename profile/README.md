### 🚀 About The Project
**Medical Cave** is a smart healthcare and emergency management platform designed to streamline hospital workflows, real-time resource allocation, and patient care coordination.

### 🧩 Core Modules
* **🏥 ICU & Incubators Management:** Live bed availability tracking across intensive care and neonatal units.
* **🩸 Blood Bank System:** Automated inventory tracking and urgent blood type matching.
* **🤖 Medical RAG AI Assistant:** Intelligent clinical decision support and automated patient history summarization.

| **`medical-cave-ai`** | RAG Engine & ML Models | Python, FastAPI, LangChain / LLMs |
